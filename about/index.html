AI Educator for Finance and Education.

I am an ACCA-qualified finance professional who explains AI from credible sources. My work sits between two worlds that rarely talk to each other: the research labs publishing what AI can actually do, and the finance and education teams deciding whether to trust it.

Most AI commentary skips the reading. I do the opposite — I go to the primary source: journal articles, technical reports, and official documentation. Then I translate what holds up into notes a busy professional can apply the same day.

That means every claim I share is checked before it goes out, evidence is cited wherever possible, and the limits are stated plainly. When something is uncertain, I say so.
